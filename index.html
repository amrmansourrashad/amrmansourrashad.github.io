About Me

As a seasoned Roads Construction Engineer at the Ministry of Transport, I apply my academic and professional expertise—anchored by a Master's degree in Highway Engineering—to advance roadway safety, operational efficiency, and infrastructure resilience across Egypt. My engineering journey commenced with a Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from Nahda University, where I also served as an Assistant Lecturer.

At the General Authority for Roads and Bridges, I contribute to the successful execution of infrastructure projects through a combination of technical proficiency, strategic contract negotiation, and effective teamwork. Currently participating in the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) in Data Analytics, I am focused on integrating modern data science techniques with traditional civil engineering to drive innovation in infrastructure development.
